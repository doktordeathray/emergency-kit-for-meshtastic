CHROMIUM BROWSER (free, open-source)
====================================
Flashing radios and connecting by USB/Bluetooth needs a Chromium-family browser
(Chrome, Edge, Brave, Chromium). Safari and Firefox can't do it.

Windows already has Microsoft Edge - nothing needed.
Mac / Linux without Chrome: start the drive and type C in the black window.
It installs from here with no internet and no admin password.

  Mac:    *_arm64-macos.dmg  (Apple chip)   *_x86_64-macos.dmg  (Intel)
  Linux:  *-x86_64_linux.tar.xz (normal PCs)   *-arm64_linux.tar.xz (Pi 4/5, ARM)

This is "ungoogled-chromium" (https://github.com/ungoogled-software), BSD-licensed:
Chromium without Google services. It may be copied and shared freely.
